# Block Plurality

**Block plurality** (also *plurality-at-large* or *block voting*) is a multi-winner rule in which each voter casts up to as many votes as there are seats, with at most one vote per candidate. The candidates with the most votes fill the seats.

## How it works
- For *k* seats, each voter may vote for up to *k* different candidates.
- Each candidate can receive at most one vote per ballot.
- The top *k* vote-getters win.

## Key characteristics
- The multi-winner extension of [[Plurality]].
- Tends to be **majoritarian**: a cohesive majority bloc can sweep all the seats, often underrepresenting minorities.
- Simple to use but offers little proportionality.
